# Back Cover

I’m Cy Dequest. I wrote of marks, their past;
Full Stop, eleven chapters, held me fast.
Two disagreed, and chapter twelve was due;
My editor still waited. Nothing new.

I built a tool, not judging prose I wrote:
A list of claims, the chapters, then a note
When any chapter clashed, it gave a quote.

When run on this, it flagged the count as wrong:
Full Stop’s eleven did not here belong.

And Otto, over-eager, brought to light
A seventeenth-century man to cite:
A man who set the type, made up outright.

This tells of months of evenings, work to shun
One book. The tool then got another done.

I write a software firm’s documentation;
My index cards in shoeboxes: location
Is known for all but one. I cannot tell:
One card behind the radiator fell.
