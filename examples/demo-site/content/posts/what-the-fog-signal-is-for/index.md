On a clear night nobody needs the fog signal, and on a foggy night nobody can see the lamp.
The two instruments split the weather between them.

## A sound has to say which sound it is

The horn at Gull Point sounds two blasts every thirty seconds.
`Horn(2) 30s` is how the chart writes it.
A captain who hears two blasts and counts thirty knows it is us and not the station down the coast.

## What fog does to a sound

Fog carries a sound farther than people expect, and it bends it in ways nobody can predict.
So the horn tells a captain which station is near, and never how far away it is.
