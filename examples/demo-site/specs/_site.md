# What the Lighthouse Log holds

## A signal has to be recognizable, not just visible {#recognizable-signal}

A light or a sound is useful only when a stranger can tell it from every
other one on the coast. Brightness and loudness come second.

## The log tells no real rescue {#no-real-rescues}

A story about a ship in trouble is a composite, and it says so, once.
The families of real crews read this log.
