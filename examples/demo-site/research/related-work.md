## The Northern Coast Pilot, 1998 edition {#coast-pilot}

The sailing directions for the northern coast, with every light's
pattern as the charts print it. The lamp post takes its description of
Gull Point from here.

## The Gull Point station logbook {#station-log}

The keepers' daily record since the station opened. Read for the winding
schedule and the fog hours, and not cited by a post yet.
