## What I expect next

A way to track claims across the whole manuscript instead of patching two chapters. I expect the software detour to start with this need, but I’m wondering whether it can help someone who won’t surrender a favorite title.

## What lost me, or what I didn't believe

“Fashion doesn't happen twice.”

Why not? Two shops contributing to the same fashion seems quite plausible, and a chapter title isn’t historical evidence. This abrupt verdict didn’t convince me that the happy-week solution failed. “Nobody wants to read about being early” also felt like an unjustified excuse to preserve the stronger story after the sources had already favored a smaller claim. I can believe the narrator is attached to the prose; I’m less persuaded that the contradiction itself is insoluble.

## More or less?

More of what the sources actually said, less of declaring which arguments a chapter is allowed to have. I liked the escalating physical mess and the circular footnotes. Not naming the shops keeps things light, but it also makes it harder to care about the historical question on its own terms.
