## What I expect next

Nothing further in this book; this feels like the ending. Beyond it, I imagine Quirework continuing and Full Stop staying in its eleven-chapter state. I don’t expect a triumphant return to finish the punctuation history, and I’m relieved the ending doesn’t force one.

## What lost me, or what I didn't believe

“This book said ‘eleven chapters’. This book has nine.”

I can count nine narrative chapters if I leave out the title page and preface, so the number itself didn’t bother me. What did is that the tool apparently mistook an explicitly different book’s chapter count for this one’s. The one-line correction is believable, but after the questionable flag in the demo it leaves me wondering how much interpretation the narrator must supply before the software’s judgments are useful. “I've stopped minding” also comes rather quickly: I accept abandoning the manuscript, but wanted a little more of how the old anxiety loosened its grip.

## More or less?

A little more of the experience of writing this book, less recap of the tool’s checking routine. The return of Red’s question, the pin holes, and the misplaced card makes a satisfying close. I liked that the detour produced something without retroactively fixing everything. Still, the finished book appears so quickly that I feel the change in the narrator’s answer more fully than the change in his evenings.
