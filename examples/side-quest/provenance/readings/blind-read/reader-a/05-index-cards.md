## What I expect next

The tool grows into Quirework, gets a name, and gets shown to other people. The title "The Book That Disagreed" suggests the tool will also find contradictions in other people's books or documents. I expect Full Stop to stay stalled.

## What lost me, or what I didn't believe

"I write about software for a living. I mention it here because it took me this long to mention it to myself." This arrives late and feels like a convenient reveal. If the narrator writes about software, a forty-line script on a Sunday is less of a leap, and I wondered why it took three years. Also "The book had never failed at the sentences" is a bit neat as an epiphany.

## More or less?

More of the shoebox and radiator material, which is tactile and funny. Less of the neat epiphany at the end. The script's fourteen findings could use one or two more concrete examples.
