Cy Dequest was writing Full Stop, a natural history of punctuation. It had eleven chapters, a shelf of books that disagreed, and an editor waiting for chapter twelve.

Instead of finishing Full Stop, they built a tool to check what the book had promised against what its chapters said. The tool had no opinion about sentences. It kept a list of claims and flagged disagreements. When they ran it on this book, it marked Full Stop's chapter count as an error.

The months of evenings went to the tool, and to Otto, an over-eager assistant who supplied a seventeenth-century compositor, complete with a name. The compositor was invented. The sentence was good. The software built to avoid a book ended up finishing this one instead.

Cy Dequest writes documentation for a software company. They keep their index cards in shoeboxes, though they couldn't say which one holds the card that fell behind the radiator.
