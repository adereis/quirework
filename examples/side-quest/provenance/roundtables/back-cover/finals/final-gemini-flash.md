Cy Dequest was writing Full Stop, a natural history of punctuation. It had eleven chapters, a shelf of disputing sources, and an editor waiting for chapter twelve.

Instead of finishing it, they built a tool to check what the book had promised against what the chapters said. The tool had no opinion about sentences. It kept a list of claims and said so when a chapter disagreed. When they ran it on the book they wrote instead, it flagged Full Stop's chapter count as an error.

This is an account of the months of evenings that went to the tool, an over-eager assistant who invented a seventeenth-century compositor, and the software built to avoid a book that finished this one instead.

Cy Dequest writes documentation for a software company. They keep their index cards in shoeboxes and know where all of them are except the one that fell behind the radiator.
