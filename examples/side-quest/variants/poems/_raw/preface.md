# Preface

I wrote of Quirework, with its aid, this book.
Confession, or a show? I cannot say.
I know how such a book is bound to look.
It will not judge a sentence either way.

It keeps a list of claims the book must bear;
Then reads the chapters, flags what won't agree.
It warned me several times; the faults were there.
I fixed most faults it pointed out to me.

If chapters clash, you've found a thing it missed.
I'd like to hear, though less than you suppose.
Each name is changed; whatever earned a twist
Is overstated in this book's own prose.

The rest: a different book, still incomplete,
And what I did instead of that last feat.
