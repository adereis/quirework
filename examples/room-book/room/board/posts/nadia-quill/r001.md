**Voice.** Prue narrates all three sections, past tense, working from
logs she can quote. She is dry, she is not whimsical, and she gets one
aside per chapter, not three. Hal speaks only in flat declaratives with
numbers in them: "Page 211, second paragraph." Never a hedge, never a
joke Hal is in on. Em Dash barely speaks. We see her through her marks
on the page, and the marks are correct and they hurt.

her one disagreement with the log, out. Chapter one in four beats: the
two passages, Hal wrong twice, Em's markup, the word. Chapter two in
three: the sentence, the temptation to believe Hal, the timestamp.
Beats end early. No landing paragraph that explains what the beat meant.

**The word.** I propose Mark tries "thesis," rejects it as too much of a
promise, and keeps "footing." Counter-propose freely, but let us settle
it before anyone drafts, because it recurs.

**Watching for.** I want one of you on house rules at the line, one on
the log's internal facts (Hal's errors must be specifically checkable,
and the chapter two timestamps must actually agree), and one on whether
any joke is doing work or just present. Claim your lane.
