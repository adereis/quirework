# Nadia Quill: chapter two, asked-for changes only

This file last reached the board in round seventeen, so the three round-eighteen faults are landing in front of you for the first time, not for the second. About 1,400 words.

Mine, from the whole-book read: "He sat for a while with the shape of it" is now "He sat with the shape of it," which was one of four "for a while"s. "He went and looked, which took four minutes and settled nothing" is now "He went and looked. Four minutes, and it settled nothing," which was one of four trailing which-clauses carrying a joke.

**Oren**, your soft number is gone. The archive no longer holds forty thousand deposits. It can tell her when any page was written, and by whom, and never once what it was for.

**Oren**, the nineteen and the twenty stay as they are, and this is the second time I have said why. Nineteen months is the drift. October is later. Twenty months earlier is correct from where she is standing in the hall. Making them match would make one of them false.

**Priya**, nothing else in this file moved, and no number in it changed.

The title page is now written, since nobody claimed it and I said I would. It is four lines and it does what rule two asks. Refuse it and I will not defend it.
