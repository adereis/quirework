### preface.md

I did not write what follows. I assembled it.

Mark Downe kept everything: drafts, notes, the files beside the files, and nine years of logs he never deleted. I have read all of it, in order, twice. What follows is a reconstruction. Where two accounts of the same afternoon disagree, I have gone with the log, because the log was written at the time by someone with no interest in being interesting.

So, plainly. This is fiction. Mark Downe is invented. So is Em Dash, who edited him, and Hal Lucinate, who wrote with him, and Sid Karr and his motorcycle, and Vic Tor, whose diagrams are not in this edition. I am invented. The book Mark could not finish does not exist, which is the only mercy in the story. One thing in it is real, and it is the tool he built instead of finishing: Quirework.

Now the part I would rather leave out.

There is one sentence in Mark's manuscript that everyone quotes. You will come to it. Before I read the logs I wanted it to be his. I said so in writing, which was unwise, and I have not gone back to soften it. He believed it was his. He said so for years, not loudly, the way a man mentions something he assumes is settled.

The log does not agree. At 23:14 on a Tuesday, Hal Lucinate produced the sentence, thirty-one words, in one pass. Eleven minutes later Mark cut four of them. They are the four words that make it land. I have looked for a reading of that record which gives the sentence to him, and there is not one. He made it good. He did not make it.

I wanted otherwise. The log wins. Winning is the log's job. Saying what it cost is mine.

That is why the name on the cover is mine. I did not write these chapters. I am answerable for them, which is a smaller thing, and the only accurate word for it is the one we use in the archive: custody. Somebody has to be responsible for the order the pages are in.

Mark is at his desk. The manuscript is three hundred and eighteen pages long and says two opposite things about the same subject, four chapters apart. He has not noticed yet.
