# SOP fragment: shared voice rules for every derived edition

Every edition of Chapter and Verse Control, however it is retold, is
still an edition of this book: keep its ideas and its actual doubts, and
never smooth a hedge into a confidence the source page did not earn.

## Blind-draft brief

Keep every idea and every hedge from the source chapter; a reader who
compresses "maybe" into "yes" has changed the book's claim, not just its
length. Do not invent a scene, a line of dialogue, or a fact the source
chapter does not contain. Write no em-dash, anywhere, for the same
reason the canonical book has none: use a colon where the second part
names the first, a comma for a looser join, or two sentences. Do not
explain a joke the source leaves for the reader to get on their own.
