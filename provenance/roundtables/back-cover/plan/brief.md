# The back cover

Write the back cover of a short book: a blurb of about a hundred and
twenty words, and one author line.

The book is in front of you. Its preface, one chapter from the middle,
and its last chapter come with your first turn. The rest of it is on
disk under `manuscript/sections/`, in reading order: How's the Book?,
Two Shops, Index Cards, Otto, The Name, Nine Hundred Lines, Em, The
Demo, Full Stop. Read as much of it as you need. The blurb should come
from the book itself.

What the cover must hold to:

- About a hundred and twenty words of blurb. A little either side is
  fine; much more is not.
- One author line. The author is Cy Dequest, whose pronouns are
  they/them, and the line says so.
- No comparable titles, no endorsements, no real books and no real
  people. Everyone in the book has a changed name, and the cover brings
  in nobody from outside it.
- No em dashes, anywhere: not the character, not three hyphens, not
  `&mdash;`. Use a colon, a comma, or two sentences.
- US spelling.
- Dry and concrete. Never explain a joke. If a sentence can go, it goes.

Keep the current draft in `shared/back-cover.md`, with the blurb first
and the author line after it, so that the file is always the cover as
it stands.
