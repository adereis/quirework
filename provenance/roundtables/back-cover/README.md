# Roundtable: the back cover

The back cover was written by a roundtable on 2026-10-09: three cheap
models on three harnesses, each seated as itself, and Cy in the last
seat, closing every round. Three rounds. The goal was a blurb of about a
hundred and twenty words and one author line saying Cy is they/them,
with no comparable titles and no real books or people.

`plan/` is the plan as it was frozen, `plan.json`, and the brief every
seat was given. Each seat was handed the preface, Otto and Full Stop in
full with its first turn, and the other seven chapters on disk. Nobody
was given the spec, the sidecars or this guide: the blurb was to come
from the book, not from a description of it. `board/board.md` is the
board as `quirework sop roundtable board` printed it, Cy's posts and the
supervisor's two notes included. `drafts/` holds the shared draft as each
seat that changed it left it, and `finals/` each seat's own final
version. All of it is evidence, not the book: nothing reflows it or
holds it to the house rules. The seats' raw records, sessions and
receipts stay in the table, outside the book.

supervisor. Gemini-Flash's receipts carry the same open red flag as the
Otto room's Antigravity seat: the session bus filtered to the keyring's
secrets service, which Antigravity needs for its login.

## The rounds

1. Gemini-Flash drafted, Luna checked, Haiku edited. Cy corrected three
   facts nobody caught: Cy is not a programmer, the three years were
   Full Stop's and the tool took months, and the pronouns belong in the
   author line by use, not in parentheses.
2. Haiku fixed them; Gemini-Flash and Luna said what they would still
   change. Two of them found the same problem, which book is which. Cy
   named Full Stop in the first sentence, took Luna's "avoid a book" and
   Gemini-Flash's "the months of evenings that went to the tool", and
   asked for the radiator joke back. Cy also corrected their own post:
   the book never says Cy works for a software company, and the author
   line keeps it anyway, as the one place the facts come from Cy.
3. Closed as the last round. Each seat gave its own final version, what
   it would still change, its strongest objection and what would change
   its mind. A supervisor's note let the versions go in `shared/`
   rather than the 200-word post.

## What became of it

Cy took Haiku's final version with two changes: Gemini-Flash's "When
they ran it on the book they wrote instead", and Cy's own "It had eleven
chapters, two of which disagreed", which retired a shelf none of the
seats had checked. Nothing else changed. It is
`manuscript/sections/back-cover.qmd`.
